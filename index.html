출원·심사·등록 절차Filing, Examination & Registration

특허·상표·디자인 권리화 절차를 간략히 안내합니다A brief guide to the patent, trademark and design process

출원은 지식재산처에 권리를 신청하는 절차이며, 심사를 거쳐 등록이 되어야 비로소 권리가 발생합니다.A filing is an application for rights submitted to the Ministry of Intellectual Property; rights arise only once the application passes examination and is registered.

특허Patent

  1. 출원
    (명세서·청구범위)    Filing
    (specification & claims)
  2. 방식심사Formality check
  3. 출원공개 · 심사청구Publication · Request for examination
  4. 실체심사
    (의견서·보정서)    Substantive examination
    (response & amendment)
  5. 특허결정Grant decision
  6. 등록료 납부
    설정등록    Fee payment
    Registration

상표Trademark

  1. 출원Filing
  2. 방식심사Formality check
  3. 실체심사
    (의견서)    Substantive examination
    (response)
  4. 출원공고
    (이의신청 기간)    Publication
    (opposition period)
  5. 등록결정Registration decision
  6. 등록료 납부
    설정등록    Fee payment
    Registration

디자인Design

  1. 출원Filing
  2. 방식심사Formality check
  3. 심사
    (또는 무심사등록)    Examination
    (or registration without examination)
  4. 등록결정Registration decision
  5. 등록료 납부
    설정등록    Fee payment
    Registration
